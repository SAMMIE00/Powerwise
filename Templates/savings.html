LIGHTING

Use lights efficiently

  • Switch off lights when leaving an empty room.
  • Use LED bulbs where possible.
  • Make use of natural daylight during the day.

COOLING

Reduce fan usage

  • Switch off fans when nobody is in the room.
  • Use an appropriate fan speed for your comfort.
  • Clean fan blades regularly.

AIR CONDITIONING

Cool rooms wisely

  • Keep doors and windows closed while cooling.
  • Clean air filters regularly.
  • Use a moderate temperature setting.

REFRIGERATION

Use your fridge efficiently

  • Avoid leaving the refrigerator door open.
  • Check that the door seals close properly.
  • Allow hot food to cool before refrigerating it.

ELECTRONICS

Cut standby consumption

  • Switch off devices when they are not needed.
  • Unplug chargers that are not in use.
  • Use power-saving settings where available.

IRONING

Plan your ironing

  • Iron several clothes in one session.
  • Sort clothes by the temperature they require.
  • Switch off the iron immediately after use.

LAUNDRY

Wash full loads

  • Wash full, appropriate loads instead of many small ones.
  • Use a suitable wash cycle.
  • Avoid unnecessary repeat washes.

KITCHEN

Cook more efficiently

  • Use a pot or appliance suitable for the task.
  • Keep lids on pots when appropriate.
  • Switch off electric cooking appliances after use.

OTHER APPLIANCES

Check your daily habits

  • Review how long each appliance runs every day.
  • Avoid leaving appliances running unnecessarily.
  • Check appliance wattage before buying replacements.
